    Clock

    Pick the UTC offset for your area - it's converted to the POSIX format the device needs automatically. Daylight saving isn't applied on its own, so switch offsets manually when it starts/ends, or pick "Custom" for a POSIX rule that handles it (e.g. PST8PDT,M3.2.0,M11.1.0 for US Pacific, CET-1CEST,M3.5.0,M10.5.0/3 for EU).

    Display

    Photo album

    Copy .bmp, .jpg or .png images into /photos on the microSD card (use the Files tab to upload them directly from here). 480x320 images look best; others are auto-scaled.

    Weather

    Uses OpenWeatherMap's free Current Weather API. Sign up for an API key, then look up your city's numeric ID with their city search (the ID is in that page's URL, e.g. ...&id=1835848 for Seoul).
